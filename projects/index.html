College thesis · Materials science

First-Principles Investigations of the Properties of 2D High-Entropy Transition Metal Dichalcogenides: (MoWVNbTa)S₂

With Allan Abraham B. Padama

In this thesis, I use first-principles calculations to investigate a two-dimensional material containing five transition metals. The study explores its atomic structure and electronic properties, including how charge is distributed between its constituent elements.

The calculated band structures and density of states suggest metallic behavior, while the cohesive energies support the stability of the investigated structures. I presented this work at the 28th SPVM National Physics Conference, where it received Best Presentation in the poster session.
