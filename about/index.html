Hi, I’m Dinan, an Applied Physics student at the University of the Philippines Los Baños. I’m curious about how the world works and enjoy exploring that question through research and technology.

Background

I’ve been fascinated by physics since high school. For me, studying physics is a way to understand the world around us and the laws that shape it.

Education and Qualifications

At UPLB, I developed an interest in materials science. My college thesis uses first-principles calculations to study a two-dimensional high-entropy transition metal dichalcogenide. I’m also interested in cybersecurity and continue to learn about it through certifications.

My first conference was the 28th National Physics Conference of the Samahang Pisika ng Visayas at Mindanao, where I presented my research poster and received Best Presentation in the poster session. I’m grateful to Sir Padama for his guidance and supervision. Read about the experience.

I’ve also enjoyed sharing what I learn with others, including serving as a lecturer for the UP Mindoreños Academic Review in Victoria, Oriental Mindoro.

Passions and Interests

Outside physics, I enjoy video games, Formula 1, technology, and programming.

I use Arch Linux and enjoy learning how computers work, from software to PC hardware. I’m also working on a browser game inspired by density functional theory, bringing together my interests in physics, programming, and games.

Goal

My immediate goal is to complete my degree. In the long term, I hope to contribute to physics and technology in the Philippines through the work I do and the things I learn.

Why This Website

I created this website to share my experiences, research, and ideas. You’ll find my CV here, along with posts about things I’ve learned and moments worth remembering.

Get in Touch

I’d be happy to connect, discuss ideas, or hear about opportunities to collaborate. You can reach me at dinanmariano@gmail.com.

Thanks for stopping by!
