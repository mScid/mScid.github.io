My First Conference — Best Presentation at SPVM

Samahang Pisika ng Visayas at Mindanao’s 28th National Physics Conference · Ateneo de Davao University

My first conference was the Samahang Pisika ng Visayas at Mindanao (SPVM) 28th National Physics Conference, held at Ateneo de Davao University from October 22–24, 2026. I presented my research in the SPVM Poster Session, and I’m proud to have received Best Presentation.

The poster presented my work, “First-Principles Investigations of the Properties of 2D High-Entropy Transition Metal Dichalcogenides: (MoWVNbTa)S₂.”

Thank you, Sir Padama, for your guidance and supervision. I’m grateful for your support throughout this work, and proud to have shared it at my first conference.
